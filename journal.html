Journal

In the pit, as in life.

A home for our philosophy, thoughts on music and community, show stories, and lessons learned from the people who make a scene what it is.

What we do here follows us.

Look out for each other. Make room for someone new. If someone falls, pick them up.

The things that make a great music community aren't so different from the things that make us better people.

The pit is more than chaos. At its best, it's a place where we let go, look out for each other, and find something bigger than ourselves.

That's the kind of community we want to build.

The first entries come with the first nights.

This journal opens as the shows do. Stories from the room, notes from artists, and what we learn by building a scene in Cleveland — and anywhere the music goes after that.
