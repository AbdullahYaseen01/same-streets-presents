The introduction

Same Streets. New Scene.

Good music deserves to be heard. Great artists deserve somewhere to grow. And everyone deserves a place where they belong.

Same Streets Presents exists to help make those things happen.

We're building a home for live music through unforgettable shows, intimate performances, artist features, and a community that believes music is worth showing up for.
